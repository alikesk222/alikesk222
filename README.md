### About Me

Security-focused developer with hands-on experience in **SOC operations**, **threat hunting**, **SIEM tools**, and **vulnerability management**.
Skilled in both offensive and defensive techniques — malware analysis, social engineering detection, packet & log analysis, and ISO 27001.

On the dev side: production-ready platforms with **PHP / Laravel**, **React / Next.js / TypeScript**, and **Python** — always with a security-first mindset.
